## ★ v1.37 Probe Results

### DebugApplicationStdArray

```
len=0
```

**CRITICAL FINDING.** Contrast with scan #18 (GS1 DataMatrix pharma, Grade C): `len=13`.

Two possible explanations:
1. **Grade F suppression**: firmware skips applicationStdArray population when the symbol
   is a total fail (DECODE=X). The GS1 parser may require a decoded codeword stream to
   populate row-by-row AI checks.
2. **Digital Link pipeline difference**: GS1 Digital Link URIs are parsed differently
   from traditional GS1 (FNC1 first position / `]d2`). The DL parser may identify the
   overall format as valid (hence `ApplicationPass=Fail(Quality)` rather than
   `Fail(Data Format)`) but not generate the per-field `applicationStdArray` rows.

**Evidence for explanation (2)**: `ApplicationPassReason=Quality` (not `Data Format`)
means the firmware recognized the DL URI as a valid GS1 format. If len=0 were purely
a Grade F artifact, we might expect `Fail(Data Format)` instead since the parser couldn't
have completed format validation.

**Evidence for explanation (1)**: The symbol is DECODE=X — the codeword stream may be
too damaged for the parser to walk field-by-field, so applicationStdArray comes back empty
even though the URL was (partially?) reconstructed.

**Resolution required**: Scan a passing or near-passing GS1 Digital Link QR symbol.
If len>0 on a good-quality DL QR → explanation (2) wrong, Grade F is the cause.
If len=0 on a good-quality DL QR → DL uses a different pipeline, no per-field table.

## Key Observations

### 1. GS1 Digital Link QR — first device-confirmed example
The firmware decoded `https://id.gs1.org/...` correctly and classified it as GS1
`ApplicationStandard=Custom`. SymbologyId=`]Q1` — same AIM ID as non-GS1 QR. The
symbology layer does not distinguish GS1 DL from plain QR; only `DecodedData` prefix
and `ApplicationStandard` indicate GS1 DL.

**VTCCP parser implication**: detect GS1 Digital Link by routing `DecodedData` starting
with `https://` through the GS1 library's Digital Link parser (already in
`vtccp/lib/gs1-syntax-engine/`). The `gs1-syntax-engine` v1.4.0 handles both traditional
GS1 encodings (FNC1/`]d2`) and DL URIs.

### 2. FormalGrade format on total fail
`0/F` — only 2 fields (numericGrade/letterGrade), not the full
`numericGrade/aperture/wavelength/lighting` notation seen on passing/grading scans.
The C# parser must handle both formats.

### 3. VIBGrade=F confirmed again on total-fail QR
Pattern grades (ULP/URP/LLP/HCT/VCT/ALP/VIB/FIB) all return `F` on total fail,
not `X` and not `'-'`. The `'-'` sentinel is only returned on *passing* v1–6 QR
symbols (no VIB block present). This is now confirmed across scans #3, #10, and #19.

### 4. ECI=(null) — Digital Link encoding does not use ECI
The HTML report shows ECI=(null). `]Q1` AIM modifier=1 was previously observed to
"indicate ECI presence" on grade-A GUID scans (scans #2, #11 etc.). This DL QR also
carries `]Q1` but has no ECI designator. Two possibilities:
- The `]Q1` modifier=1 does NOT reliably indicate ECI presence on all QR symbols
- The ECI field is null because of total decode failure (ECI segment unrecoverable)

An ECI-bearing DL QR (e.g. encoded with explicit ECI 000003) would settle this.

### 5. DecodedData populated despite Grade F
The URL was fully recovered and is present in push XML. The decoder can extract data
even from a symbol it cannot fully grade. This confirms the firmware's decode and grade
pipelines are independent — a passing `DecodedData` does NOT imply a passing `DecodeGrade`.
