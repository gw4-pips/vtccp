## v1.29 probe results — QR-specific findings

### DebugSymbols

```
symbols=null
```
**CONFIRMED on QR too**: `q.symbols` null on fw 6.1.16_sr4 regardless of symbology.

### DebugAimId
```
id=]Q1;len=3;qrBranch=1;symName=QR;
```
- `qrBranch=1` ✓ — QR branch correctly fires
- AIM ID `]Q1` confirmed for this QR code

**CRITICAL FINDING — AIM modifier does NOT encode ECLevel:**  
Our v1.28 assumption was `bits[1:0] of modifier = ECLevel (0=M,1=L,2=H,3=Q)`.  
Actual result: modifier=1, but DMST shows ECLevel=**M**.  
If bits[1:0]=01 → L (our assumption), that contradicts DMST.  

The `]Q{n}` modifier encodes **ECI presence**, not EC level:
- ]Q0 = QR Code, no ECI
- ]Q1 = QR Code with ECI (000003 confirmed in DMST)

**ECLevel cannot be derived from the AIM modifier.** The v1.29 AIM decode logic for ECLevel is wrong and must be removed.

### DebugValidationGS1

**ecLvl=absent on QR also.** `r.validation.errorCorrectionLevel` does not exist
