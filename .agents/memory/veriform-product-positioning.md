---
name: VeriForm product boundary
description: Product-family positioning for canonical reports, RFID validation, and Command Pilot.
---

VCCS VeriForm is a focused standalone product that produces current-format verification reports from supported verifiers. It must provide full value without RFID or the complete Command Pilot platform.

VCCS RFID VeriWedge is the enhanced offering. It may include VeriForm GS1 Report Template output and add clearly separate RFID cross-validation evidence.

Command Pilot remains the broader integration and operator platform that can bring VeriForm, FlexWedge, and RFID VeriWedge together.

**Why:** A focused reporting product is easier for VCCS to explain, sell, deliver, and support, while preserving a natural upgrade path to RFID and full workflow orchestration.

**How to apply:** Keep canonical barcode reporting independent of RFID dependencies. Describe RFID as an optional supplement or enhanced offering, never as a prerequisite for VeriForm.
